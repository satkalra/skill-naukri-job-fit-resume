## Routing rule

When it's unclear which variant fits a job description, use the JD's first-listed responsibilities:

- **Customer-facing or delivery-facing responsibilities listed first** → Variant A.
- **System-building / platform / infrastructure responsibilities listed first** → Variant B.
- **Domain-or-data-as-the-product responsibilities listed first** → Variant C.

If the JD doesn't clearly match any pattern, default to the variant matching the most recent role actually held, and say which one was picked and why.
